gmail-mcp

A personal helper tool. Not a public service.

gmail-mcp is a small set of command-line scripts that one person runs on their own computer to work with their own Google account: send and find e-mail in Gmail, add events to Google Calendar, and read or update the files and spreadsheets the tool itself works with in Google Drive and Google Sheets.

Who can use it

Only its owner. The tool is not offered to other people, has no sign-up, and does not work with anyone else's Google account.

What it asks Google for

Access to the owner's Gmail (read, send and organise messages), Google Calendar, Google Sheets, and the Google Drive files that the tool creates or opens. Nothing else.

Privacy

По-русски

Это не публичный сервис: у него один пользователь, его владелец. Политика конфиденциальности.
